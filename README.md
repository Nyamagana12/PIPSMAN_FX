
# PIPSMAN FX 🚀

PIPSMAN FX ni professional Forex trading application iliyoundwa kwa traders wanaotaka kupata market analysis, trading setups, premium signals na elimu ya Forex sehemu moja.

## 📱 Main Features

- 🔥 Premium Forex Signals
- 📊 Daily Market Analysis
- 🎯 High-Quality Trading Setups
- 📚 Forex Trading Education
- 🎥 Educational Videos
- 📈 Market Watchlist
- 🔔 Signal Notifications
- 👤 User Profile
- 💎 Premium Membership

## 📊 Trading Analysis

PIPSMAN FX itawapa traders:
- Market direction
- Entry zones
- Stop Loss (SL)
- Take Profit (TP)
- Risk management
- Technical analysis
- Trading setups

## 📚 Education

Sehemu ya Education itakuwa na:
- Forex basics
- Price Action
- Smart Money Concepts (SMC)
- Risk Management
- Trading Psychology
- Advanced Trading Strategies
- Video lessons
